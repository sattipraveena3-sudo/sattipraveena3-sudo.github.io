Current research ✦01 / Medical AI

ReliEMCAD

A reliability-gated lightweight test-time adaptation framework for medical segmentation. It estimates shift and predictive uncertainty, then chooses freeze, selective adaptation, or referral; the design includes acceptance testing, rollback, calibration, and harmful-adaptation analysis.
