Reproducibility study · 2026

EMCAD Synapse Verification + ReliEMCAD

Verified the released PVT-EMCAD-B2 checkpoint across all 12 official Synapse test volumes with CPU-compatible batched inference. Observed 83.86% mean Dice and 74.68% mean Jaccard, with 0 missing and 0 unexpected checkpoint keys. Proposed ReliEMCAD for reliability-gated lightweight test-time adaptation under distribution shift.
