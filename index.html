Frozen research direction

One problem, three connected contributions.

The central problem is evidence-induced instability in retrieval-augmented generation. Every core research step measures, explains, or mitigates that problem.

01 / Measure

Track answer trajectories

Measure correct→incorrect reversals, incorrect→correct repairs, persistence, recovery, and first-reversal depth across fixed nested retrieval rankings.

EARBCRRTB
02 / Explain

Diagnose why evidence hurts

Separate effects of depth, retrieval relevance, contradiction, order, context position, and the model’s use of supported versus unsupported evidence.

RetrievalConflictFailure Analysis
03 / Mitigate

Verify only when answers change

Test a low-cost evidence-stability gate that accepts a better-supported correction, retains a safer prior answer, or abstains when support is insufficient.
