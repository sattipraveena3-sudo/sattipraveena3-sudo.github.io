Research proposalPath A extension

ReliEMCAD: Reliability-Gated Lightweight Test-Time Adaptation

Proposes a deployment-time framework for medical segmentation that estimates distribution shift and predictive uncertainty, then chooses freeze, selective adaptation, or referral instead of adapting every target input unconditionally.

In a 148-slice Synapse volume pilot, a controlled gamma intensity shift reduced Dice from 72.59% to 53.78% and Jaccard from 63.64% to 46.62%, motivating uncertainty-aware adaptation, acceptance testing, rollback, calibration, and harmful-adaptation tracking.

Research agenda

Doctoral questions I want to pursue.

01 / Medical AI

When should a model adapt?

Reliable test-time adaptation under scanner, intensity, and deployment shift with explicit uncertainty, calibration, referral, acceptance testing, and rollback.

02 / Retrieval + Language

How can retrieval-grounded models become more dependable?

Study sparse/dense retrieval, ranking behavior, evidence attribution, metric reliability, and system-level evaluation for knowledge-intensive language systems.

03 / Multimodal + Systems

How should AI reason and act under incomplete evidence?

Multimodal/time-series learning and verification-aware systems that preserve uncertainty, traceability, controlled execution, and documented failure modes.
