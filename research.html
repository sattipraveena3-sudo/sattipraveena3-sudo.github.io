Flagship questionFrozen · Fall 2027 focus

Evidence-Induced Answer Reversals in RAG

When retrieved evidence is expanded along a fixed ranking, which answer changes are harmful, why do they persist or recover, and can a change-triggered evidence-stability gate reduce correct-to-incorrect transitions without suppressing incorrect-to-correct repairs?

The unit of analysis is one question’s answer trajectory over nested Top-k evidence. This exposes individual failures that aggregate accuracy can hide.

Contribution plan

Three stages with one causal thread.

01 / Benchmark

Measure trajectories, not just Top-k accuracy

Use fixed nested rankings, deterministic generation, open QA datasets, multiple retrievers and model families, bootstrap confidence intervals, and public result contracts.

02 / Diagnose

Explain harmful and beneficial changes

Study relevance, contradiction, passage order, context position, and evidence support; distinguish persistent reversals from temporary errors that later recover.

03 / Gate

Verify only on observed answer change

Compare the old and new answers against evidence, then accept, retain, or abstain. Evaluate reversal reduction together with beneficial corrections, accuracy, coverage, and cost.
